## Method notes

- Spearman uses average ranks for ties; with only two label levels present it is a rank-biserial-type correlation.
- 95% CIs: percentile bootstrap, 2000 resamples of cases, fixed seed; resamples where the statistic is undefined are skipped.
- Proxy comparators: top 3 by popularity (ties by rank) among brands seeded by the other side's entity and filtered to the proxy tags; not by rank, to avoid a built-in senior->junior overlap.
- No language model is used anywhere in this harness.
