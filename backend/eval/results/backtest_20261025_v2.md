## Deviations from pre-registration

On 2026-10-25, after run 1 (`backtest_20261025.md`, kept unchanged as the record of the pre-registered harness), three rules were changed. Each fixes a measurement error that was visible without looking at the labels:

1. **Universe rule.** Pre-registered: a brand's category universe was its top-5 insights tags (`filter.tag.types=urn:tag:genre:brand`). Those tags describe what the brand's *audience* likes, not what the brand is (a sneaker brand came out as "Food & Beverage"). Now: the brand's own `urn:tag:genre:brand` tags from its /search result, most specific nodes only (ancestors dropped, depth >= 2 preferred, up to 3). The insights-tags rule remains only as the fallback for a brand without own brand-genre tags.
2. **Censored ranks.** Pre-registered: a target not found among the first 150 brands counted as rank 151, i.e. percentile 0. Now such a rank is reported as censored (no rank percentile), and the target's affinity is looked up directly (`filter.results.entities=<target>`, same seeds, filter and type). This gives a second signal, the affinity ratio = target affinity / affinity of the top-ranked brand (0..1).
3. **Taxonomy synonym.** "test preparation" / "educational materials" mapped to `academic` (universities); they now map to `entertainment:publisher` (partial).

Labels (`cases_candidates.csv`) and `mapping.csv` are unchanged (same mapping sha256). Run 1 is kept as the record; the v2 numbers are post-hoc and should be read as such.

## Method notes

- Spearman uses average ranks for ties; with only two label levels present it is a rank-biserial-type correlation.
- 95% CIs: percentile bootstrap, 2000 resamples of cases, fixed seed; resamples where the statistic is undefined are skipped.
- Proxy comparators: top 3 by popularity (ties by rank) among brands seeded by the other side's entity and filtered to the proxy tags; not by rank, to avoid a built-in senior->junior overlap.
- No language model is used anywhere in this harness.
